### Agenda

This edition is a hands-on workshop around the [Bitcoin Dev Kit](https://bitcoindevkit.org).
Together we will:

- Build a sample wallet using BDK ([bitcoindevkit.org](https://bitcoindevkit.org)) By [sdmg15](https://github.com/sdmg15)
  - Connect it to our testnet
  - Broadcast a transaction
  - Sync with the blockchain and check the balance
- Analyzing Mining Pool Behavior to Address Bitcoin Core's Double Coinbase Reservation Issue (By [78Bessala](https://github.com/78Bessala))
- Exploring Bitcoin Improvement Proposals with [bips.dev](https://bips.dev/) (By [nejos97](https://github.com/nejos97))
- Q&A and discussion

**Please come with your computer** and start going through the [BDK documentation](https://bitcoindevkit.org/docs/) in the meantime.
